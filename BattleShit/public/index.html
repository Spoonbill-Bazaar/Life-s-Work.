// NAVAL THEATRE COMMAND  ·  IRREGULAR WATERS  ·  2 OPERATORS

Both fleets hide in the same theatre: odd-shaped waters with islands, reefs and dead space. You may only place and fire on open water.

Each turn: optionally launch your commander's ultimate, then fire one shot. Firing ends your turn.

Ultimate charge: +1 every turn, +1 for every hit you take, +1 more when you lose a ship. Taking damage brings your ultimate sooner.

Keys: Q arm ultimate · R/right-click rotate · Esc cancel · M mute
